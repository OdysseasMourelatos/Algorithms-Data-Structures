A collection of algorithmic and data structures assignments developed as part of the
**Algorithms & Data Structures** course in the **Department of Management Science and Technology (DMST)** at the **Athens University of Economics and Business (AUEB)**.

The repository contains implementations and solutions covering fundamental concepts in algorithms, data structures, algorithm design, computational problem solving, and complexity.
